## 1. AI Strategy & Governance

### 1.1 UAE National Strategy for Artificial Intelligence 2031

- **Access link (official PDF):** <https://ai.gov.ae/wp-content/uploads/2021/07/UAE-National-Strategy-for-Artificial-Intelligence-2031.pdf>
- **Portal page:** <https://u.ae/en/about-the-uae/strategies-initiatives-and-awards/strategies-plans-and-visions/government-services-and-digital-transformation/uae-strategy-for-artificial-intelligence>
- **Abstract:** The UAE's flagship AI strategy, launched by the UAE Office for AI, aims to make the country a global leader in AI by 2031. Eight strategic objectives: build AI-destination reputation, deploy AI across priority sectors, develop the ecosystem, adopt AI across government services, attract and train talent, bring world-leading research to target industries, provide data and infrastructure as a test-bed, and ensure strong governance and effective regulation. Targets AED 335 bn in AI-driven economic growth aligned with UAE Centennial 2071.
- **Referenced in rows:** 1, 10 (governance & R&D objectives).

### 1.2 UAE Charter for the Development and Use of Artificial Intelligence (July 2024)

- **Access link (official portal):** <https://uaelegislation.gov.ae/en/policy/details/the-uae-charter-for-the-development-and-use-of-artificial-intelligence>
- **PDF mirror:** <https://www.govx.digital/hubfs/The%20UAE%20Charter%20for%20the%20Development%20and%20Use%20of%20Artificial%20Intelligence.pdf>
- **Abstract:** A voluntary ethics charter issued by the UAE Office for AI, Digital Economy and Remote Work Applications on 10 June 2024. Sets out 12 ethical principles for AI development and use — including human dignity, algorithmic bias mitigation, data privacy, transparency, human oversight, governance & accountability, technological excellence, human commitment, peaceful coexistence with AI, inclusive access, and compliance with applicable laws and treaties. Serves as the guardrail reference for Agentic-AI deployment.
- **Referenced in rows:** 10.

### 1.3 UAE Cabinet Resolution (18 May 2026) — Federal Framework for Agentic AI Project + 80,000-employee Training Programme

- **Access link (UAE Government Media Office):** <https://mediaoffice.ae/en/news/2026/may/18-05/mohammed-bin-rashid-chairs-uae-cabinet-meeting>
- **Alt coverage (The National):** <https://www.thenationalnews.com/news/uae/2026/05/18/uae-to-train-80000-government-workers-in-agentic-ai-under-high-tech-drive/>
- **Abstract:** Cabinet-approved framework to transition ≥50% of federal government services and operations to Agentic-AI models within two years, coupled with the largest-ever federal training programme — 80,000 employees in five categories (Leadership, Technical, Specialist, General Workforce, Train-the-Trainers). Establishes governance roles across all ministries, forms per-entity work teams headed by the respective minister, and defines KPIs for the transition. Anchors "UAE Government 4.0".
- **Referenced in rows:** 10.

### 1.4 Dubai Universal Blueprint for Artificial Intelligence (2024)

- **Access link (UAE portal):** <https://u.ae/en/about-the-uae/strategies-initiatives-and-awards/strategies-plans-and-visions/government-services-and-digital-transformation/dubai-universal-blueprint-for-ai>
- **Abstract:** Launched by H.H. Sheikh Hamdan bin Mohammed, Crown Prince of Dubai, in April 2024. A yearly plan to accelerate AI adoption across the emirate, aligned with the Dubai Economic Agenda D33 (target: AED 100 bn contribution/year, 50% productivity uplift). First-phase initiatives include appointing a Chief AI Officer in every Dubai government entity, launching AI & Web3 incubators, an AI Week in educational institutions, fast-tracked land for data centres, and a new commercial AI licence.
- **Referenced in rows:** 10 (Dubai-specific extension).

## 2. Data Protection & Privacy

### 2.1 UAE Federal Decree-Law No. 45 of 2021 — Personal Data Protection Law (PDPL)

- **Access link (official legislation portal):** <https://uaelegislation.gov.ae/en/legislations/1972>
- **Direct PDF (English mirror):** <https://protection-data.ae/wp-content/uploads/2023/10/federal-decret-law-45-uae.pdf>
- **UAE portal overview:** <https://u.ae/en/about-the-uae/digital-uae/data/data-protection-laws>
- **Abstract:** UAE's landmark federal data-protection law, issued 20 Sep 2021, in force 2 Jan 2022. Establishes an integrated framework for confidentiality of personal information — collection, use, storage, processing and cross-border transfer. Applies across the UAE excluding free-zones. Defines controls for data controllers/processors, general obligations, consent requirements (Art. 5–9), sensitive-data categories including health (Art. 12), data-subject rights (Art. 22), DPIAs (Art. 20), and automated-decision-making (Art. 24).
- **Referenced in rows:** 8, 9, 10.

### 2.2 Dubai Data Law — Law No. (26) of 2015 Regulating Data Dissemination and Exchange in the Emirate of Dubai

- **Access link (Dubai portal):** <https://www.dubai.ae/en/pages/dubai-data-law-26-of-2015.aspx>
- **Alt (Dubai Data Establishment):** <https://www.dubaipulse.gov.ae/organisation/dubai-data-establishment/service/data-law>
- **Abstract:** Dubai emirate-level law classifying data into four categories (open, shared, confidential, sensitive) and mandating data-sharing between government entities. Establishes the Dubai Data Establishment as regulator. Forms the legal backbone for emirate-level open-data and cross-agency exchange.
- **Referenced in rows:** 9.

### 2.3 UAE Federal Law No. 2 of 2019 on the Use of ICT in Health Fields ("Health Data Law")

- **Access link (official legislation portal):** <https://uaelegislation.gov.ae/en/legislations/1288>
- **Portal overview (MOHAP):** <https://mohap.gov.ae/en/services/electronic-health-services>
- **Abstract:** Federal law regulating the use of ICT and electronic communications in the UAE health sector. Governs generation, storage and cross-emirate/cross-border transfer of health data, licensing of digital health services, and data-privacy safeguards specific to healthcare. Provides the legal envelope inside which AI clinical decision-support and federated-learning designs must operate.
- **Referenced in rows:** 8, 9.

### 2.4 UAE Open Data Policy — Federal (TDRA) + Open Data Specifications Guidelines

- **Access link (TDRA):** <https://tdra.gov.ae/en/open-data/open-data-policy>
- **DGov Specifications:** <https://dgov.tdra.gov.ae/en/publications/guidelines-for-open-data-specifications>
- **Portal overview:** <https://u.ae/en/about-the-uae/digital-uae/data/open-government-data>
- **Abstract:** Federal open-data policy issued by TDRA. Sets the "open by default" principle for federal datasets, mandates machine-readable formats (CSV / JSON), permissive re-use licence with attribution, and defines quality/metadata specifications. Applied by all federal entities.
- **Referenced in rows:** 1.

## 3. Digital Government & Telecom Regulation

### 3.1 UAE Digital Government Strategy 2025

- **Access link (UAE portal):** <https://u.ae/en/about-the-uae/strategies-initiatives-and-awards/strategies-plans-and-visions/government-services-and-digital-transformation/uae-national-digital-government-strategy>
- **TDRA-DGov page:** <https://dgov.tdra.gov.ae/en/publications/the-uae-strategy-for-government-services>
- **Abstract:** Federal strategy for the digital transformation of UAE government by 2025. Anchored on 8 principles: inclusive-by-default, resilient, fit-for-the-digital-age, user-driven, digital-by-design, data-driven, open-by-default, and proactive. Targets 100% digital-maturity across federal + local entities. Operationalised by TDRA and its Digital Government arm (DGov).
- **Referenced in rows:** 2.

### 3.2 TDRA Telecom Quality of Service (QoS) Regulatory Framework

- **Access link (TDRA regulations catalogue):** <https://tdra.gov.ae/en/about-tdra/legal-references/laws-and-regulations>
- **Abstract:** Regulatory framework issued by TDRA that mandates minimum service-quality thresholds for licensed telecom operators. Covers KPI reporting (DL throughput, uplink throughput, latency, jitter, packet loss, drop-call rate), consumer-protection service-level clauses, and audit rights. Foundation for any AI-driven QoS-prediction and SLA-assurance system.
- **Referenced in rows:** 2, 5.

### 3.3 UAE 5G Rollout Policy & TDRA 5G Framework

- **Access link (TDRA):** <https://tdra.gov.ae/en/media/publication>
- **Portal overview:** <https://u.ae/en/about-the-uae/digital-uae/mobile-technology/5g-in-the-uae>
- **Abstract:** Federal 5G rollout policy setting minimum coverage percentages, urban/rural throughput obligations and standardisation alignment for 5G deployment in the UAE. Provides the regulatory context for beam-level traffic forecasting and SON/RAN AI use cases.
- **Referenced in rows:** 4.

### 3.4 TDRA National Frequency Plan (NFP)

- **Access link (TDRA):** <https://tdra.gov.ae/en/telecom/telecom-regulation/spectrum-management>
- **Abstract:** TDRA-issued national plan for spectrum allocation across UAE, including mmWave and beam-forming allocations. Governs which frequency bands and beam configurations are available to operators, and therefore the constraints under which AI-based beam-traffic forecasters operate.
- **Referenced in rows:** 4.

### 3.5 UAE Digital Economy Strategy

- **Access link (UAE portal):** <https://u.ae/en/about-the-uae/strategies-initiatives-and-awards/strategies-plans-and-visions/economic-strategies/uae-digital-economy-strategy>
- **Abstract:** National strategy to double the digital economy's GDP contribution over ten years. Includes measurable KPIs on 5G availability, digital-service experience and digital-skills penetration; provides the outcome frame for many telecom-AI use cases.
- **Referenced in rows:** 2, 4.

## 5. Business Continuity & Resilience

### 5.1 AE/SCNS/NCEMA 7000:2021 — Business Continuity Management Standard

- **Access link (NCEMA portal):** <https://www.ncema.gov.ae/en/media-center/publications/publications.aspx>
- **PDF (NCEMA):** <https://www.ncema.gov.ae/vassets/48c1c32a/Publication-en.pdf.aspx>
- **Prior version 2012 PDF:** <https://www.ncema.gov.ae/content/documents/BCM%20English%20NCEMA_29_8_2013.pdf>
- **Abstract:** UAE's national standard for Business Continuity Management Systems (BCMS), issued by the National Emergency Crisis and Disasters Management Authority (NCEMA). Requires organisations to conduct risk analysis, produce BCM plans, define Recovery Time Objectives (RTO), and demonstrate resilience against disruptions (natural disasters, cyberattacks, pandemics). Applicable to all UAE entities including government. Matches the predict → alert → recover workflow for radio-link-failure ML.

- **Access link (NCEMA):** <https://www.ncema.gov.ae/en/media-center/publications/publications.aspx>
- **Abstract:** NCEMA guidance on escalation channels and communication protocols during crisis events (including telecom outages). Prescribes how early-warning alerts from predictive systems should be routed to the NOC, emergency services and the public.
- **Referenced in rows:** 5.

- **Access link (UAE portal):** <https://u.ae/en/about-the-uae/strategies-initiatives-and-awards/strategies-plans-and-visions/environment-and-energy/uae-energy-strategy-2050>

- **Abstract:** First unified UAE energy strategy, launched 2017 and updated 2023 to align with Net Zero 2050. Aims to triple renewable-energy contribution, invest AED 150-200 bn by 2030, raise clean-energy share from 25% to 50% by 2050, and cut power-generation carbon footprint by 70% (~AED 700 bn savings). KPI: grid emissions intensity (gCO₂/kWh) — used by the energy-scheduling ML notebook.

- **Abstract:** In October 2021, the UAE became the first MENA country to commit to net-zero by 2050. The strategic initiative defines sectoral emissions ceilings including for telecom/ICT, roadmaps for hydrogen and solar, and is expected to create ~200,000 jobs across solar, battery and hydrogen sub-sectors.
- **Referenced in rows:** 3.

## 7. Food Security & Agriculture

### 7.1 National Food Security Strategy 2051

- **Access link (UAE portal — strategies):** <https://u.ae/en/about-the-uae/strategies-initiatives-and-awards/strategies-plans-and-visions/environment-and-energy/national-food-security-strategy-2051>
- **Alt (UAE portal — federal strategies):** <https://u.ae/en/about-the-uae/strategies-initiatives-and-awards/federal-governments-strategies-and-plans/national-food-security-strategy-2051>
- **Abstract:** Launched Nov 2018 by MoCCAE. Targets top-place in the Global Food Security Index by 2051 (and top-10 by 2021). Defines a national food basket of 18 main types with 3-5 alternative supply sources each. Key pillars: boost domestic food production, strengthen local manufacturing, drive innovation & private capital into agri-tech, and secure international acquisitions/partnerships. Pillar 2 explicitly enables "technology-based production of food" — the anchor for precision-agriculture ML.

- **Abstract:** Abu Dhabi's agri-food regulator publishing SOPs for farm registration, monitoring, biosecurity, plant/animal health, food-safety inspection and traceability. Governs how ML-derived cropland maps could be operationalised at farm level.
