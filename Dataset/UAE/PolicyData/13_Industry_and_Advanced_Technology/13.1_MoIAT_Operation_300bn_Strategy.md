## Abstract

10-year industrial strategy — raise industrial-sector GDP contribution from AED 133 bn to AED 300 bn by 2031. Supports 13,500+ SMEs. Priority manufactured products: space tech, med-devices & pharma, clean/renewable energy, machinery, chemicals, metals, advanced-tech manufacturing, electronics, food & beverages.
