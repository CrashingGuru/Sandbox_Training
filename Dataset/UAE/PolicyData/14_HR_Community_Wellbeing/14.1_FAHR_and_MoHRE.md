## Abstract

FAHR is the federal HR authority — the signing counterparty to MBZUAI for the 80 k Agentic-AI Programme. MoHRE regulates private-sector labour, Emiratisation quotas, and workforce-development programmes; publishes cybersecurity/HR-tech guidance.
