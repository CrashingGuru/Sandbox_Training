## Abstract

EHS is the first regional entity certified to ISO 42001:2023 (AI Management System). Three-pillar AI strategy: (i) integrate AI across healthcare + community-health systems, (ii) empower national talent, (iii) advance secure & reliable infrastructure. Includes 'EHS Intelligence' central AI platform and 'Amal' — UAE's first multi-agent AI Physician Assistant.
