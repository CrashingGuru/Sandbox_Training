## Abstract

SEHA is Abu Dhabi's public-hospitals operator; Malaffi is Abu Dhabi's Health Information Exchange (equivalent of Dubai's NABIDH), storing unified EHRs across the emirate. Operational context for federated-learning across emirate hospitals.
