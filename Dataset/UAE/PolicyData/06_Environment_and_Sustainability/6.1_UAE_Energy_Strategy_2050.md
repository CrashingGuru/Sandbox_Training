## Abstract

First unified UAE energy strategy (2017), updated 2023 to align with Net Zero 2050 — triple renewables, invest AED 150-200 bn by 2030, raise clean-energy share to 50 % by 2050, cut power-generation carbon footprint by 70 % (~AED 700 bn savings).
