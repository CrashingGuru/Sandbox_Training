Our Services

Our services are designed with our customers in mind. Our goal is to provide you with an efficient and professional customer service experience.

  • Student Self-Services Portal

    Parents and students can quickly access a range of services, including signing parent contract, attesting educational certifications, applying for education continuation certificate, and more.

  • Education Providers Services Portal

    Education providers can apply for education permit services, advertisement approvals, school fees regulations, school services, and more.
