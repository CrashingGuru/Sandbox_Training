## Abstract

From AY 2025-26 the UAE became one of the first countries to make AI mandatory from kindergarten through Grade 12 across public schools (~300 k pupils). Curriculum spans 7 areas: foundational concepts, data & algorithms, software use, ethical awareness, real-world applications, innovation & project design, policies & community engagement. Delivered inside Computing / Creative Design / Innovation without extending school hours; taught by 1,000+ specially-trained teachers; no written exams — practical projects only.
