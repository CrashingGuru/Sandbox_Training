# TDRA Telecom Quality of Service (QoS) Regulatory Framework

- **Issuing body:** TDRA

## Abstract

Mandates minimum service-quality thresholds for licensed telecom operators — DL/UL throughput, latency, jitter, packet loss, dropped-call rate — plus consumer-protection SLAs and audit rights. Foundation for any AI-driven QoS-prediction system.
