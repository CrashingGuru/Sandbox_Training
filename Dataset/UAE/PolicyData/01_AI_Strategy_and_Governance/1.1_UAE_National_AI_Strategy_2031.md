## Abstract

UAE's flagship AI strategy — eight strategic objectives: AI-destination reputation, priority-sector deployment, ecosystem development, government-services adoption, talent, world-leading research, data & infrastructure test-bed, governance & regulation. Targets AED 335 bn in AI-driven economic growth aligned with UAE Centennial 2071.
