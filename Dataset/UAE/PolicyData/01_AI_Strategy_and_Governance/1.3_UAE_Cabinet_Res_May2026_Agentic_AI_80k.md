## Abstract

Approves ≥50 % transition of federal services/operations to Agentic AI within two years; establishes governance roles across all ministries; launches the 80,000-employee Federal Agentic-AI Skills & Capabilities Programme (Leadership, Technical, Specialist, General Workforce, Train-the-Trainers).
