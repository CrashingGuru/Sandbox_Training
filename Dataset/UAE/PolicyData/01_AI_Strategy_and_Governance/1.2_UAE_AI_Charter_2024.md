## Abstract

Voluntary ethics charter with 12 principles — human-machine ties, safety, bias mitigation, data privacy, transparency, human oversight, governance & accountability, technological excellence, human commitment, peaceful coexistence, inclusive access, legal compliance. Serves as the guardrail reference for Agentic-AI deployment.
