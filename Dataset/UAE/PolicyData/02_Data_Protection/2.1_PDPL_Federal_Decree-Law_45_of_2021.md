## Abstract

Federal data-protection law (issued 20 Sep 2021, in force 2 Jan 2022). Establishes an integrated framework for confidentiality — collection, use, storage, processing, cross-border transfer. Applies across the UAE excluding free-zones. Key articles: Art. 5-9 (lawful bases), Art. 12 (sensitive/health data), Art. 20 (DPIAs), Art. 22 (data-subject rights), Art. 24 (automated decision-making).
