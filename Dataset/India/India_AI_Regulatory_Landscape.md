# India — AI Regulatory & Policy Landscape

> Consolidated reference list of Indian regulations, policies, missions and
> guidance instruments relevant to (1) AI in 6G/5G, (2) multilingual LLMs,
> (3) RAG usage, (4) data privacy, (5) cross-border data usage, and (6) the
> KSA hackathon gap-register crosswalk. **Telecom / networks first.**

---

## 1. AI usage in 6G / 5G — telecom-anchored stack

### 1.1 Telecommunications Act, 2023
- **What it is:** India's foundational telecom law, replacing the Indian
  Telegraph Act 1885, the Indian Wireless Telegraphy Act 1933 and parts of the
  TRAI Act 1997.
- **Relevance:** enabling framework for AI, IoT and blockchain in telecom;
  stricter cybersecurity and data-localisation on TSPs; streamlined spectrum
  management for 5G / 6G; rule-making power for AI-enabled fraud detection.
- **Text (India Code):** <https://www.indiacode.nic.in/handle/123456789/20101>
- **Explainer (Wikipedia):** <https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Telecommunications_Act,_2023>
- **Invest India explainer:** <https://www.investindia.gov.in/blogs/telecommunications-act-2023-propelling-innovation-and-growth>
- **Securiti — Telecom Data Regulations:** <https://securiti.ai/data-regulations-in-india-telecom-sector/>

### 1.2 Bharat 6G Vision Document (DoT, March 2023)
- **What it is:** the DoT vision that positions India for global 6G leadership
  by 2030.
- **B6GA (Bharat 6G Alliance) home:** <https://www.bharat6galliance.com/>

- **Bharat 6G Vision Document (on B6GA site):**
  <https://bharat6galliance.com/bharat6G/Home/content/Bharat-6G-Vision-Document/Bharat-6G-Vision-Document>

  <https://bharat6galliance.com/bharat6G/public/assets/report/DPR-for-Bharat-6G-Roadmap-2030.pdf>
- **B6GA Brochure (PDF):**
  <https://bharat6galliance.com/bharat6G/public/front_assets/pdf/Brochure-Bharat-6G-Alliance.pdf>

### 1.3 Bharat 6G 2026 initiative (Bharat Exhibitions)
- <https://www.bharatexhibitions.com/en/B6G2026/index.html>

### 1.4 TRAI Recommendations on "Leveraging AI and Big Data in the Telecommunication Sector" (2023)
- Proposes an AI-and-Data Authority of India, a fair-AI framework, sandboxes
  for use-case testing, voluntary industry codes.
- <https://www.trai.gov.in/node/370>

### 1.5 TRAI Telecom Commercial Communication Customer Preference (Third Amendment) Regulations, 2026
- Binding rules: ML/AI-based spam identification, mandatory AI-caller
  disclosure, consent verification, restricted 140/160 number series,
  penalties up to ₹10 lakh.
- Includes joint TRAI–RBI pilot on digitally verifiable consumer consent.
- **News:** <https://techobserver.in/news/telecom/trai-third-amendment-spam-call-regulations-2026-321411/>
- **TRAI 2026 AI-Calling compliance guide:** <https://qcall.ai/trai-updates-for-ai-calling>
- **TRAI Regulations index:** <https://www.trai.gov.in/release-publication/regulations>
- **TRAI Directions index:** <https://www.trai.gov.in/release-publication/directions>

### 1.6 Operationalisation of the Telecommunications (Telecom Cyber Security) Rules under the 2023 Act
- Telecom-sector cybersecurity, incident reporting, Network Function Testing.
- **Whalesbook note:** <https://www.whalesbook.com/news/English/telecom/India-Moves-Telecom-to-New-Authorization-Framework-Under-2023-Act/6a3be76fa1c5bf08432ad2f6>

## 2. Multi-lingual LLM

## 3. RAG (Retrieval-Augmented Generation)

> India does not (yet) have a RAG-specific rule; RAG is governed by a stack
> of adjacent instruments.

### 3.1 India AI Governance Guidelines, MeitY (5 November 2025)
- Seven Sutras (Do No Harm, Fairness, Transparency, Accountability,
  Inclusivity, Privacy, Sustainability); six pillars of governance; explicit
  guidance on grounding generative outputs to mitigate misinformation and
  hallucination — closest thing to a RAG-quality expectation in India today.
- **PIB press release:** <https://www.pib.gov.in/PressReleasePage.aspx?PRID=2186639>
- **PIB PDF (guidelines full text):** <https://static.pib.gov.in/WriteReadData/specificdocs/documents/2025/nov/doc2025115685601.pdf>
- **NeGD press release:** <https://negd.gov.in/press_release/meity-unveils-india-ai-governance-guidelines-under-indiaai-mission-to-ensure-safe-inclusive-and-responsible-adoption-of-artificial-intelligence-across-sectors/>
- **Chambers and Partners — decoding:** <https://chambers.com/articles/meity-unveils-india-s-approach-towards-regulating-artificial-intelligence>
- **Saikrishna & Associates — decoding:** <https://www.saikrishnaassociates.com/decoding-the-india-ai-governance-guidelines/>
- **SCC Times summary:** <https://www.scconline.com/blog/post/2025/11/06/meity-launches-india-ai-governance-guidelines-under-indiaai-mission-2025/>
- **AI Data Analytics Network:** <https://www.aidataanalytics.network/data-science-ai/news-trends/india-unveils-new-ai-governance-guidelines-to-encourage-responsible-adoption>
- **Anevagi summary:** <https://www.anevagi.com/article/meitY-issues-india-AI-governance-guidelines-2025.php>

### 3.2 IT (Intermediary Guidelines and Digital Media Ethics Code) Amendment Rules, 2026
- India's first binding rules defining "synthetically generated information"
  (SGI); intermediaries must label SGI and attach machine-readable metadata;
  3-hour takedown & safe-harbour tests.
- **March 2026 rules — India Policy Hub:** <https://indiapolicyhub.in/2026/02/19/india-ai-regulatory-framework-2026-sgi-3-hour-takedown-safe-harbour/>
- **May 2026 amendment — India Policy Hub:** <https://indiapolicyhub.in/2026/05/18/india-ai-content-moderation-rules-2026/>

### 3.3 Sectoral overlays that push RAG-with-citations for regulated verticals
- **RBI Master Direction on IT Governance, Risk, Controls and Assurance Practices, 2023** — auditability, verifiable outputs.
- **SEBI Circular on AI/ML use in Securities Markets (2019, updated periodically)** — auditability.
- **DPDP Act — purpose-limitation & consent on retrieved personal data used in RAG indexes** (see §4).

## 4. Data privacy

### 4.1 Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 (DPDP Act)
- India's principal personal-data law; extraterritorial reach; consent
  regime; Significant Data Fiduciary designations; children's data
  (parental consent, no behavioural targeting/tracking); DPB India;
  penalties up to ₹250 crore. Draft DPDP Rules published January 2025;
  staged notifications through 2026.
- **§16 text and interpretation:** <https://www.dpdpa.com/dpdpa2023/chapter-4/section16.html>
- **AI compliance angle — AIRiskAware:** <https://airiskaware.com/insights/india-dpdp-act-ai-compliance>
- **India AI Policy overview — AIRiskAware:** <https://airiskaware.com/india-ai-policy>
- **DPO India — DPDP impact on cross-border:** <https://www.dpo-india.com/Blogs/impact-dpdpa-cross-border/>
- **DPO India — reconciling with global regimes:** <https://www.dpo-india.com/Blogs/cross-border-data-transfers/>

### 4.2 Sector overlays stricter than the DPDP baseline
- **Telecom Cyber Security Rules (under Telecommunications Act 2023):** CDR /
  subscriber-data / incident data must be stored in India.
- **RBI Storage of Payment System Data Directive (2018):** payment data must
  be stored only in India (mirror allowed post-processing).
- **NDHM Health Data Management Policy (2023) / draft DISHA Act:** sensitive
  health-data classification with stricter transfer conditions.

### 4.3 Aggregated legal overview
- **Prashant Mali — AI Laws and Regulations in India (2026):** <https://www.prashantmali.com/cyber-law-blog-india/ai-laws-and-regulations-in-india-as-of-2026>
- **Gyansetu — Government AI Policy Guide (2026):** <https://www.gyansetu.in/blog/government-ai-policy-in-india/>

## 5. Cross-border data usage

### 5.1 DPDP Act §16 — "negative-list" (blacklist) model
- Personal data transfer allowed to any country *except* those the Central
  Government notifies as restricted; sector-specific stricter rules prevail.
- **DPDP §16 text:** <https://www.dpdpa.com/dpdpa2023/chapter-4/section16.html>
- **Securiti — DPDPA cross-border requirements:** <https://securiti.ai/cross-border-data-transfer-requirements-under-india-dpdpa/>
- **Lexology — Compliance Guide for Global Businesses:** <https://www.lexology.com/library/detail.aspx?g=ce5725ab-e3c8-4070-855d-0519e36dead9>
- **Legal500 — Rules, Restrictions, Compliance Roadmap for 2027:** <https://www.legal500.com/intelligence/india/privacy/cross-border-data-transfers-under-indias-dpdp-act-rules-restrictions-and-compliance-roadmap-for-2027>
- **ITIF — India cross-border data transfer regulation:** <https://itif.org/publications/2025/06/09/india-cross-border-data-transfer-regulation/>
- **IAPP — Why delocalizing data helps:** <https://iapp.org/news/a/why-de-localizing-data-helps-india-s-position>

### 5.2 Draft DPDP Rules 2025 — Rule 14
- Significant Data Fiduciaries must comply with Government orders restricting
  cross-border transfers of specified categories of personal data / traffic
  data.

### 5.3 Sector-specific residency mandates
- Telecom (CDR/subscriber/traffic data), Finance (RBI 2018 payment-data
  localisation), Health (NDHM HDM Policy). See §4.2.

### 5.4 Analysis
- **Vidhi Legal Policy — Cross-Border Data Transfers and Data Localization:** <https://vidhilegalpolicy.in/blog/cross-border-data-transfers-and-data-localization-mandate-under-the-data-protection-regime/>

## 6. KSA gap-register → India crosswalk (telecom-first)

| KSA gap theme (from KSA v3 Gap Register) | Nearest Indian instrument(s) |
|---|---|
| Advisory vs binding AI-ethics instruments (SDAIA AI Ethics Principles) | India AI Governance Guidelines (MeitY, Nov 2025) — also advisory; TRAI Fair-AI Framework recommendations remain non-binding. |
| National ↔ international standards crosswalks & drift monitoring | TEC standards + TRAI-driven Fair-AI Framework — references 3GPP / ITU-T / ISO/IEC 42001 but no maintained crosswalk with drift monitoring. **Gap.** |
| Policy-node enforcement semantics (advisory vs blocking) — Y.3172 | No India-specific instrument today; TRAI AI/ML-in-Telecom recommendations propose a sandbox where policy-node semantics could be tested. **Sandbox opportunity.** |
| Consent, purpose-limitation, residency for sensitive/minors' data | DPDP §9 (children) + Telecom Cyber Security Rules + RBI/SEBI/NDHM overlays. India arguably stricter than KSA on minors. |
| Secondary use / targeted advertising on citizen data | DPDP §7 (purpose limitation) + §9 (children) + IT Rules 2021/2026 + TRAI TCCCPR Third Amendment 2026. |
| Fabricated / unverifiable regulatory citations (LLM hallucination) | IT Rules 2026 SGI amendment + India AI Governance Guidelines (Transparency & Accountability Sutras). No RAG-verification standard yet. |
| Localisation (Arabic-language / accessibility) | Bhashini, BharatGen, AdiVaani + Rights of Persons with Disabilities Act 2016 + GIGW 3.0 accessibility guidelines. |
| Cross-border data & residency | DPDP §16 blacklist + Telecom Cyber Security Rules + RBI 2018 payment-data localisation + NDHM. |
| Node-by-node Y.3172 governance mapping | TEC + NWG-13 (National Working Group of ITU-T SG13) — India's route to feed governance requirements back into Y.3172. |
| Energy-sector algorithm audits (Wafir gap) | CEA Cyber Security Regulations 2021; BEE AI-guidance drafts (2025). **Gap:** no mandatory algorithm-audit standard. |
