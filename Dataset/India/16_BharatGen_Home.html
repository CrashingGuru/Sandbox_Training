Featured Initiatives

Bharat Data Sagar: The World’s Largest Indian Data Repository

What it is? BharatGen is building the world’s largest dataset of its kind, focusing on underrepresented Indian data—text, speech, and images tied to languages, culture, history, and philosophy.

Why it matters? This secure, versioned corpus (e.g., 15,000+ hours of annotated voice data across 22 Indian languages by Q4 2025) empowers AI to understand and serve India’s diverse communities, from rural dialects to urban contexts.

Impact for You: Access cutting-edge AI insights, support data sovereignty, and drive innovation in your sector—whether governance, agriculture, or education.

Models and Benchmarks: Pioneering AI for India

What it is? BharatGen is developing state-of-the-art AI models for conversational AI, automatic speech recognition (ASR), text-to-speech (TTS), translation, and more, tailored to Indian Knowledge Systems (IKS) and cultural perspectives.

Why it matters? By Q4 2025, expect models for 22 Indian languages, benchmarks for education, agriculture, and law, and open-source releases to foster innovation—ensuring AI reflects real-world Indian performance.

Impact for You: Leverage these models to build applications that resonate with India’s unique needs, from smart governance to sustainable farming solutions.

Upskilling India: Building the AI Talent Pipeline

What it is? BharatGen is investing in India’s future by funding MTech/PhD researchers, launching AI courses, and hosting hackathons and workshops—aiming to make India a global AI leader, not just a consumer.

Why it matters? By Q4 2025, we’ll have empowered 40+ students through internships, launched two AI courses, and hosted two hackathons, fostering a skilled workforce inspired by sustainability and tech.

Impact for You: Join our ecosystem as a researcher, student, or partner to shape AI innovation and gain hands-on expertise for a sustainable India.

Ecosystem and Applications: Co-Creating Impact with Partners

What it is? BharatGen is building an ecosystem of partners—startups, system integrators, government agencies, and VCs—to co-create AI applications for good governance, defense, agriculture, and commercial use.

Why it matters? By Q4 2025, we’ll deploy models to real-world applications, generate revenue through licensing, and collaborate with organizations like Bhashini to maximize impact across India.

Impact for You: Partner with us to develop transformative solutions, co-create intellectual property, and reach millions with sustainable, India-centric AI.

BharatGen Open Source: Sharing Knowledge for a Sustainable Future

What it is? BharatGen will release a subset of models, weights, recipes, and data as open source, starting with text and TTS models in 2025, while selectively sharing advanced resources with government and trusted partners.

Why it matters? This initiative democratizes AI innovation, enabling developers and researchers to build on BharatGen’s work, rooted in India’s cultural heritage and sustainability goals.

Impact for You: Access world-class AI tools for free or through partnerships, driving innovation in your projects while aligning with Bharat’s values.
