About Us

Revolutionizing Industrial Supply & Logistics











Amer is Saudi Arabia's integrated B2B digital trade platform, connecting suppliers, buyers, and transport companies across 8 industrial sectors — construction materials, mining, petrochemicals, landscaping, equipment rental, laboratories, project surplus, and scrap &amp; recycling.

We eliminate the complexity of traditional procurement: the repeated phone calls, the opaque pricing, the unreliable middlemen. On Amer, every transaction is transparent, traceable, and fully protected.

Our Mission

We strive to build the Kingdom's most trusted industrial marketplace — where any Saudi business can source what it needs, ship it reliably, and pay with complete confidence.

We operate in alignment with Vision 2030's drive to digitize the Saudi economy and empower local businesses at scale.

What Makes Us Different


Licensed by the Ministry of Transport &amp; Logistics and the Ministry of Commerce
Registered with D-U-N-S and the Saudi Authority for Intellectual Property
ZATCA-compliant e-invoicing on every transaction
Escrow payments — funds are only released upon confirmed delivery
Live across Saudi Arabia and the GCC
Free to join for suppliers, buyers, and transporters

Amer operates two live apps: the Amer Material Service app for buyers and suppliers, and the Amer Driver app for transport companies — a dedicated, purpose-built experience for every party in the ecosystem.

For Buyer
Centralized and Simplified Purchasing: Instead of dealing separately with multiple suppliers and different transport companies, Amer Platform provides a unified digital marketplace. You can search for all the materials you need, order them, arrange their transportation, and pay for them — all through one easy-to-use platform. This saves you significant time and effort.

What Makes Amer Different?

Unified Marketplace

It combines the process of buying materials and booking their transportation into a single, integrated digital platform, eliminating the need to use separate services.

Increased Efficiency

It saves significant time and effort by replacing traditional, manual processes (like phone calls and site visits) with a streamlined, app-based workflow.

Cost Reduction

It helps users save money by providing transparent pricing, allowing for easy comparison between different suppliers and offers.

Complete Control & Tracking

It gives you end-to-end control over your orders, from purchase and invoicing to real-time shipment tracking through the driver application.

Enhanced Trust & Transparency

It builds a trustworthy ecosystem through a rating and review system for all parties, ensuring reliability and quality.

Accessibility & Ease of Use

It offers a simple, user-friendly mobile experience, allowing you to manage all procurement and logistics needs from anywhere, at any time.
