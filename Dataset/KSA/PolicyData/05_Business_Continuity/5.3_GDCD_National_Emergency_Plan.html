About Civil Defence

more

Civil Defense, as defined by the Civil Defense Law issued by Royal Decree No. M/10 dated 10/5/1406 H (20/1/1986), is a set of measures and actions necessary to protect the population as well as public and private property against the hazards of fires, disasters, wars, and other incidents, in addition to providing relief to victims, maintaining the safety of transportation, communications, and the proper operation of public utilities, and protecting national resources at times of peace, war, and emergencies.

Consulting Engineering Offices

To register and obtain official accreditation from the General Directorate of Civil Defense as an engineering consultancy or technical services office specializing in fire prevention and protection, you may access and complete the registration through the Salamah Portal via the following link: https://salamah.998.gov.sa/#/landing For further inquiries, you may contact technical support at 920000356. To inquire about the engineering consultancy offices and technical service offices accredited by the General Directorate of Civil Defense in the field of fire prevention and protection, you may view the list through the following link: https://salamah.998.gov.sa/#/landing/engineers-offices
