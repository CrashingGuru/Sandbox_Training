## 4. Provenance notes

### 4.1 What the provenance tags mean

- ✅ **Official PDF** (8 files) — authoritative document fetched from the issuing entity's CDN. Includes 3 SDAIA PDFs (Ethics Principles, GenAI Guidelines, Adoption Framework), 3 NCA cybersecurity PDFs (ECC-2:2024, Guide-to-ECC, CSCC-1:2019), 1 NCA CCC PDF (via Wayback of the direct file), 1 SDAIA PDPL Handbook.
- 📄 **Official portal HTML** (~13 files) — live ministry / authority page. Full policy PDF may not be publicly downloadable, but the portal page carries the abstract and key clauses.
- 🕰️ **Wayback snapshot** (~28 files) — retrieved via `archive.org` because the source domain is geoblocked from non-KSA IPs. `if_/` URL-modifier used to strip the Wayback toolbar wrapper.
- ⚖️ **Third-party summary** — used where the primary is Cloudflare-protected + no Wayback exists; see MANUAL_DOWNLOADS.md § "Suggested substitutes".

## 5. Notes on differences with UAE

- **No emirate-level split** — KSA is fully federal. No parallel Dubai/Abu Dhabi regulators means no `SEHA` vs `EHS` split, no `Malaffi` vs `NABIDH` HIEs, no `DHA` vs `DoH`.
- **SDAIA is a super-authority** — combines AI, PDPL and NDMO under a single body (in UAE these are split between the AI Office, MoJ/UAE Data Office, and TDRA respectively).
- **NCA controls are much heavier** — 5 mandatory control families (ECC, CSCC, CCC, DCC, OTCC) vs UAE's NESA IA Standards (fewer categorical distinctions).
- **DGA leads the digital-government agenda** — its GovTech Maturity Index rank (#1 in 2025) is the analogue of UAE's Government Excellence Model 7-star system.
- **Vision 2030 realisation programmes** (HSTP, HCDP, NIDLP, NTP, QoL) sit at a strategic layer above individual ministry policies — the KSA equivalent of UAE's "Strategy 2031 + Centennial 2071" packaging.
