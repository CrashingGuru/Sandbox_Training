## Abstract

Saudi Arabia's flagship national AI strategy. Articulates a nationwide ambition to transform the Kingdom into a global hub for data and AI by 2030. Six strategic pillars: **Ambition**, **Competencies**, **Policies**, **Investment**, **Innovation**, and **Ecosystem**. Targets: top-15 nation in the AI Global Index by 2030; ~USD 20 bn in AI-driven GDP contribution; 20,000 AI/data specialists trained; 300+ AI/data start-ups launched. NSDAI positions SDAIA as the national steward of the AI + data agenda, reporting directly to the Prime Minister, coordinating cross-ministry programmes and issuing all downstream ethics, adoption and generative-AI guidance.

## Key provisions

- **Data as a strategic national asset** — under NDMO governance
- **AI ethics + regulation** — later operationalised via SDAIA AI Ethics Principles v2 (2023) and GenAI Guidelines for Government (2024)
- **Talent** — feeds MISK, KAUST, KSGAII (King Salman International AI Academy)
- **Ecosystem** — Global AI Summit as convening platform; supports the Data Embassy / Global AI Hub concept (2025 draft law)
