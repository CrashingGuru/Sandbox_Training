## Abstract

Companion to the Generative AI Guidelines for Government — this one targets **developers, deployers and end-users** of generative AI in the private sector and public spaces. Non-binding, risk-based guidance built on seven principles (fairness, reliability & safety, transparency & explainability, accountability, privacy & security, humanity, social & environmental benefit). Provides risk-based use-case classification (permitted / permitted-with-controls / prohibited) and expectations for developers on training-data provenance, evaluation, watermarking of AI-generated content, and disclosure to end-users.

## Key provisions

- **Content authenticity + watermarking** — required for AI-generated media, aligned with GCAM Digital Content Regulations
- **Consent + transparency** — users must be informed when interacting with an AI system
- **Prohibited use-cases** — impersonation, manipulation of protected groups, systems that cannot meet the seven principles
- **Vendor accountability** — obligations on foundation-model providers (bias-testing, red-teaming, third-party audit friendliness)
