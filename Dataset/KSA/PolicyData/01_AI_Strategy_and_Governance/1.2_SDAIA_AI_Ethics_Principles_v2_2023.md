## Abstract

Twelve ethics principles for AI in Saudi Arabia — fairness, privacy, security, reliability, safety, transparency, interpretability, accountability, responsibility, humanity, social & environmental benefit. Non-binding but authoritative; enforced through SDAIA guidance.
