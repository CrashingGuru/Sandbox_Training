It is an umbrella for the programs and products provided by the Human Resources Development Fund, which allow the provision and exchange of guidance, training and empowerment services efficiently and effectively to increase the stability and development of the workforce in the Saudi labor market.

HRDF Programs

Counselling

Products that assist the targeted segments in making informed decisions regarding their educational and professional paths, supporting them with planning skills for their career trajectories that align with their personal ambitions and the needs of the job market

View Program
Training

The training and qualification of national cadres to enhance their competitiveness, elevate their efficiency in the job market, and contribute to the development of their cognitive and professional skills and capabilities

View Program
Enablement

Products enabling sustainable employment for groups facing challenges in entering or staying in the job market, with the aim of increasing their participation, in addition to the initiatives provided by the fund for business owners and entrepreneurs
