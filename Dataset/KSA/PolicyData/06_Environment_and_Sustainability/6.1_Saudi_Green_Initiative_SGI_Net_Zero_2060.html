About Saudi Green Initiative Day 2026

Five years of collaborative action, driving tangible transformation

Saudi Green Initative Day is a national day to celebrate the efforts and achievements of everyone that has been working towards a greener future for the people of Saudi Arabia.

Read more about our vision
Our Mission:

Shaping the path to a sustainable future

Climate action, energy security and economic prosperity must be treated equally. The Saudi and Middle East Green Initiatives integrate environmental protection, energy transition and innovative sustainability programs to achieve a green future.
