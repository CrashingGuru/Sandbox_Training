Naama Portal

Naama is the unified electronic services portal for the Ministry of Environment, Water and Agriculture in the Kingdom of Saudi Arabia. It provides beneficiaries with all electronic services across the Ministry's various sectors (environment, water, agriculture, livestock, land, fisheries, etc.).
