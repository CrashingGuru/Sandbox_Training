Empowering 1,000,000 Citizens in AI

“SAMAI” initiative, launched by the Saudi Data and Artificial Intelligence Authority (SDAIA), aims to equip one million male and female citizens with skills and knowledge in data and artificial intelligence technologies. The initiative seeks to build a better future in which technology enhances human capabilities, while training participants on the skills and tools needed to effectively and safely integrate AI into work and everyday life.
