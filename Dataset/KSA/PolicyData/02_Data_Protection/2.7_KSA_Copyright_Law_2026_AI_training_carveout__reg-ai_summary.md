# KSA Copyright Law 2026 — AI-training carve-out — summary

- **Doc ID:** 2.7 (new — surfaces during regulations.ai coverage; not in original manifest)
- **Type:** Royal Decree, Ministry of Media / Saudi Authority for Intellectual Property (SAIP)
- **Published:** 13 February 2026
- **In force:** 1 August 2026
- **Referenced in notebook row(s):** 10, 1, 8

## Abstract

New Saudi Copyright Law issued February 2026, coming into force **1 August 2026**. Notably, it explicitly **permits reproduction of original works for the purpose of developing artificial intelligence products, without the author's permission or compensation**. This is a materially different position from the EU's Text-and-Data-Mining opt-out regime (Art. 4 of Directive 2019/790) and from the US case-law-based fair-use analysis. Positions KSA as one of the most AI-training-permissive jurisdictions globally.

## Why this matters

- **Row 10 (LLM + RAG on Saudi regulatory PDFs)** — training and fine-tuning on copyrighted Saudi regulatory / academic / news material is expressly permitted, provided the output is an AI product. This is a legal-jurisdiction reason to host LLM training on KSA-territory compute (see 1.6 Global AI Hub Law + G42 UAE + Aramco Digital sovereign clusters).
- **Row 1 (Crop Recommendation)** — training on copyrighted MEWA / ADAFSA reports is likewise permitted.
- **Cross-cutting caveat** — the carve-out permits **training** but does not immunise **outputs** that reproduce protected expression verbatim; retrieval-with-attribution (RAG) remains the safer pattern for downstream deployment.

## Interaction with other instruments

- **PDPL (2.1)** — still applies to any *personal* data inside the training corpus; the copyright carve-out does not override data-protection obligations.
- **SDAIA GenAI Guidelines Gov & Public (1.3, 1.3c)** — still require transparency, watermarking, attribution.
- **Draft Global AI Hub Law (1.6)** — Data-Embassy tenants may or may not enjoy this carve-out depending on choice-of-law selected for their embassy.
