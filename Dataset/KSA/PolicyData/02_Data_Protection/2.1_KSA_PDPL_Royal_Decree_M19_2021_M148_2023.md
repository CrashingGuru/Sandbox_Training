# Personal Data Protection Law (PDPL) — Royal Decree M/19 of 2021, amended by M/148 of 2023

- **Doc ID:** 2.1
- **Issuing body:** SDAIA (data-protection regulator)
- **Referenced in notebook row(s):** 8, 9, 10
- **Target filename (once downloaded):** `2.1_KSA_PDPL_Royal_Decree_M19_2021_M148_2023.pdf`

## Abstract

Saudi federal data-protection law (in force 14 Sep 2023 with 12-month grace to 14 Sep 2024). Purpose limitation, data-subject rights, controller obligations, registration, sensitive-data controls (health), cross-border transfer, and penalties.
