## Abstract

Implements Article 29 of the Personal Data Protection Law (PDPL). Sets the rules, safeguards and procedures for **cross-border transfers of personal data out of Saudi Arabia**. Introduces adequacy assessments for destination countries, Standard Contractual Clauses (SCCs) modelled loosely on the EU / GCC equivalents, Binding Corporate Rules (BCRs), and exceptional-circumstances derogations. The regulation also enumerates categories of data that cannot be transferred out of the Kingdom (or require additional safeguards) — health data, biometric data, genetic data are treated as extra-sensitive.

## Key provisions

- **Adequacy list** — SDAIA maintains a list of jurisdictions deemed adequate; transfers to non-adequate countries need extra safeguards
- **SCCs** — mandatory template for controller-to-controller and controller-to-processor cross-border transfers
- **BCRs** — recognised for intra-group transfers; must be approved by SDAIA
- **Derogations** — explicit consent, contract performance, public interest, vital interests, judicial claims
- **Sensitive data** (health, genetic, biometric, children's data, judicial data) — additional documentation and SDAIA notification required
- **Reporting** — annual transfer register; DPIA if transfer is high-risk
