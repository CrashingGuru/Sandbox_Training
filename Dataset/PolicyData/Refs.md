## 2. Data Protection & Privacy — MoJ, UAE Data Office, CSC

### 2.1 UAE Federal Decree-Law No. 45 of 2021 — Personal Data Protection Law (PDPL)

- **Access link (official legislation portal):** <https://uaelegislation.gov.ae/en/legislations/1972>
- **Direct English-mirror PDF:** <https://protection-data.ae/wp-content/uploads/2023/10/federal-decret-law-45-uae.pdf>
- **UAE portal overview:** <https://u.ae/en/about-the-uae/digital-uae/data/data-protection-laws>
- **Issuing body:** UAE Federal Government (Ministry of Justice / UAE Data Office).
- **Abstract:** Federal data-protection law (issued 20 Sep 2021, in force 2 Jan 2022). Establishes an integrated framework for confidentiality — collection, use, storage, processing, cross-border transfer. Applies across the UAE excluding free-zones. Key articles: Art. 5-9 (lawful bases), Art. 12 (sensitive/health data), Art. 20 (DPIAs), Art. 22 (data-subject rights), Art. 24 (automated decision-making).
- **Referenced in rows:** 8, 9, 10.

- **Issuing body:** Government of Dubai (Dubai Data Establishment).
- **Abstract:** Dubai emirate-level law classifying data into four categories (open, shared, confidential, sensitive) and mandating data-sharing between government entities. Legal backbone for emirate-level open-data and cross-agency exchange.
- **Referenced in rows:** 9.

### 2.3 UAE Federal Law No. 2 of 2019 on the Use of ICT in Health Fields ("Health Data Law")
